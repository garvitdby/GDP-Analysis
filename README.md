# GDP and Economy Analysis - Tableau Project

## 📊 Project Overview
A comprehensive Tableau-based analysis of GDP and economic indicators across 18 countries, examining economic disparities, workforce dynamics, and strategic planning opportunities.

## 👥 Authors
- **Gaurav Bhardwaj**
- **Garvit Dubey**

## 🎯 Introduction
In an era of globalization, this project provides crucial insights into economic dynamics through comprehensive analysis of GDP and various economic indicators across diverse nations. The study goes beyond traditional metrics to explore HDI, sectoral contributions, trade dynamics, and innovation indices.

## ❓ Problem Statements
1. **Economic Disparities**: Analyzing GDP variations and contributing factors
2. **Global Competitiveness**: Strategies for enhancing international positioning
3. **Policy Impact**: Examining government spending effects on growth and stability
4. **Gender Inclusion**: Exploring economic indicators and gender-related factors
5. **Innovation Development**: R&D's role in economic growth and employment
6. **Trade Dynamics**: Impact of international trade and FDI on job markets
7. **R&D Contribution**: High-tech job opportunities through research development

## 📈 Key Visualizations
### 1. GDP Mapping
- Geographical representation of economic output distribution
- Purpose: Visualize economic disparities across countries

### 2. HDI Analysis
- Human Development Index mapping
- Purpose: Understand overall population well-being

### 3. Sectoral Contributions
- Breakdown of agriculture, industry, and services sectors
- Purpose: Analyze economic composition by industry

### 4. Trade Dynamics
- Import/export balances and FDI visualization
- Purpose: Understand international economic relationships

### 5. Innovation Metrics
- R&D budgets vs patent filings correlation
- Purpose: Assess innovation landscape and tech job opportunities

### 6. Demographic Analysis
- Population categorization and age-related employment trends
- Purpose: Identify workforce dynamics and potential challenges

### 7. Gender Economics
- Female labor participation and gender pay gap analysis
- Purpose: Examine gender inclusivity in workforce

## 🔍 Key Observations
1. **GDP vs GDP per Capita**: Distinct metrics requiring separate consideration
2. **Ireland Case Study**: Significant GDP-GNP disparity due to multinational corporations
3. **FDI Patterns**: Developed countries attract more foreign investment
4. **HDI Correlation**: Strong relationship between HDI and GDP per capita
5. **R&D Impact**: Positive association between R&D budgets and economic development
6. **Age Dynamics**: Higher median age in developed countries suggesting 'ageism' implications
7. **Immigration Cycle**: Mutually reinforcing relationship between economic growth and immigration
8. **Gender Economics**: Positive impact of women's labor participation on GDP
